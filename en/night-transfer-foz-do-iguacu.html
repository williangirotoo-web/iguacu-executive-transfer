24-Hour Night Transfer in Foz do Iguaçu

At night, finding a car in Foz can be hard, especially after a show or when your flight is delayed. With a pre-booked transfer, the driver is already waiting for you.

Service 24 hours a day

We operate 24 hours a day, seven days a week. That applies to bookings on WhatsApp and, more importantly, to the service itself. We pick up and drop off passengers at any time of night, with advance booking.

For travellers, this matters at two moments: when a flight lands late, or is delayed, and when you need to leave very early to catch the first flight of the day. In both cases you do not have to rely on luck to find a car.

The price is fixed when you book, just like at any other time of day. Send us the date, time and addresses on WhatsApp; even at night, we usually reply quickly.

Late arrivals at the airport

Evening arrivals at Foz do Iguaçu airport are common, and delays happen. When you book, we track your flight in real time. If it is one, two or three hours late, the driver adjusts and is in the arrivals hall when you come out.

After a long day of travelling, the last thing you want is to haggle over a fare or wait in an empty car park. The driver meets you with a name sign, helps with your luggage and takes you straight to your hotel.

The same applies if you land at Puerto Iguazú airport (IGR) on the Argentine side on an evening flight.

Dinners, shows and nightlife

Foz do Iguaçu has plenty going on at night: restaurants with food from many countries, dinner shows with music and dance, the evening show at the Triple Frontier Landmark and bars downtown.

With a night transfer, you enjoy the evening without worrying about who will drive back. The driver takes you, waits or collects you at the agreed time, and you can order that bottle of wine with dinner.

For couples and groups who want more than one stop in the same evening, such as dinner followed by a show, booking the driver by the hour is usually the most practical option.

A night across the border

Many visitors cross the border in the evening to have dinner in Puerto Iguazú, on the Argentine side, famous for its steaks and wine. Others visit the casinos that operate on the Argentine and Paraguayan sides, where gambling is legal.

In these cases, a pre-booked transfer has an extra advantage: on the way back, late at night, you do not depend on a local taxi to cross the bridge back into Brazil. The same car that took you brings you back to your hotel in Foz.

Take your passport with you. Border controls operate at night too.

Safety and peace of mind

At night, safety weighs even more when choosing transport. With a pre-booked transfer, you know in advance who is picking you up, in which car and at what time. You do not need to wait on the pavement or get into an unknown car.

The driver waits near the exit of the restaurant, the show or the airport and lets you know on WhatsApp when they arrive. If you are running a little late, they wait.

For solo travellers, and for visitors who do not speak Portuguese, this predictability is one of the main reasons guests give for choosing a transfer.

Early departures and first flights

If your flight leaves early in the morning, we schedule the hotel pick-up with enough time for check-in. The driver arrives before the agreed time and confirms on WhatsApp, so you can come down only when they message you.

We also do early-morning departures for travellers who want to be first in line at an attraction in the high season, or who are continuing by road to other cities in Brazil, Paraguay or Argentina.

Payment can be made in Brazilian reais or US dollars. To book, send us the date, time, pick-up address and destination, or your flight number.
