Questions

Questions about night transfers in Foz do Iguaçu

Is there a night surcharge?

No. The price at night is the same as during the day, fixed when you book. Only special dates (New Year, Carnival, Holy Week and national holidays) have a surcharge.

What if my flight lands after midnight?

No problem. We track your flight and the driver waits at arrivals. The first hour after landing is free.

Can I book a dinner in Puerto Iguazú?

Yes. The round trip from your hotel in Foz costs from R$ 1.350 per vehicle, with pick-up at the agreed time.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

After we confirm availability, we send a payment link on WhatsApp: Pix, card or PayPal. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before.
