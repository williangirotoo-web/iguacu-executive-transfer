Triple Frontier Landmark Tour with your driver waiting until the end

The Landmark is where Brazil, Argentina and Paraguay meet, at the junction of the Iguaçu and Paraná rivers. We pick you up at your hotel in the late afternoon, wait during the sunset and the show, and take you back to your hotel or to a restaurant.

The visit

Sunset over three countries

From the viewpoint you see Paraguay and Argentina on the other banks of the rivers. The sunset is the busiest moment; after dark there is an evening show. Check the schedule and tickets on the official Landmark website before you go.

A popular combination: Itaipu in the afternoon and the Landmark at the end of the day.

Good to know

Dinner after the show?

Tell us when you book and the driver takes you from the Landmark to a restaurant in Foz do Iguaçu. For dinner on the Argentine side, see the dinner in Puerto Iguazú option on the booking page.

Questions

Questions about the Triple Frontier Landmark tour

How much is the Triple Frontier Landmark tour?

From R$ 1.500 in a Standard car, per vehicle and not per person, with the round trip and waiting time. The Landmark ticket is paid separately.

What time should I go?

Arrive about an hour before sunset. The time changes during the year; we suggest the pick-up time when you book.

Is the show every day?

Not always. Check the schedule on the official Landmark website before booking.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

After we confirm availability, we send a payment link on WhatsApp: Pix, card or PayPal. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before.
