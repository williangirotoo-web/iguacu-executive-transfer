The visit

Visiting the Brazilian side

The entrance to Iguaçu National Park is on Rodovia das Cataratas, about 20 to 30 minutes from downtown hotels. Inside, the park bus takes you to the falls trail: a little over 1 km of viewpoints facing the falls and the walkway that goes out over the river, near the Devil’s Throat.

The visit usually takes 2 to 3 hours. The Bird Park is right across from the park entrance and takes another 1.5 to 2 hours, which is why most people choose the option with the driver at their disposal.

Driver’s tip

Best time to go

Arriving close to opening time means shorter lines and morning light on the falls. In July, December, January and on holidays, buy your ticket in advance on the official website.

Bring a rain jacket or clothes that can get wet: the Devil’s Throat walkway is covered by the mist of the falls.

Questions

Questions about the Iguazu Falls transfer

How much is a transfer to Iguazu Falls?

Drop-off and pick-up costs from R$ 800 and a driver at your disposal for up to 5 hours from R$ 1.300, per vehicle, not per person.

How long does the visit to the Brazilian side take?

On average 2 to 3 hours, including the park bus, the trail and the walkway. With the Bird Park, add 1.5 to 2 hours.

What if I stay longer than 5 hours?

You can add extra hours when booking: R$ 100 per hour in a Standard car and R$ 125 in an Executive Sedan.

Are park tickets included?

No. The transfer includes the vehicle, driver, fuel and waiting time. Tickets for the National Park and the Bird Park are bought separately.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

After we confirm availability, we send a payment link on WhatsApp: Pix, card or PayPal. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before.
