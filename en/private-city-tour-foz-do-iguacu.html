Itinerary

What you can see in one day

A popular plan: Iguazu Falls (Brazilian side) in the morning, lunch, the Buddhist Temple or the Mosque in the afternoon and the Triple Frontier Landmark at sunset. Another option swaps the Falls for Itaipu.

You decide the order and how long to stay at each place. Check opening days and times of each attraction before you go: some close on certain weekdays.

Good to know

City tour or full day with driver?

The city tour includes up to 8 hours for attractions in Foz do Iguaçu. If you want to go further, to Argentina or Paraguay, or need more time, the full day with driver includes 10 hours and 200 km.

Questions

Questions about the private city tour

How much is a private city tour?

From R$ 1.200 in a Standard car, per vehicle and not per person, with up to 8 hours of driver at your disposal. Extra hours cost R$ 100 per hour in a Standard car and R$ 125 in an Executive Sedan. Tickets are paid separately.

Can I choose the itinerary?

Yes. Tell us what you want to see when you book, and we suggest the best order.

Are tickets included?

No. The tour includes the car, the driver, fuel and the time at your disposal. Tickets to attractions are paid separately.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

After we confirm availability, we send a payment link on WhatsApp: Pix, card or PayPal. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before.
