What having a private driver means

With a private driver, you stop thinking about how to get anywhere. Send us your travel plans and we organise every trip: the airport pick-up, the excursions, dinners, shopping in Paraguay and the ride back for your flight home.

Whenever possible, the same driver stays with you for your whole stay. They learn your pace, know what time you like to leave the hotel and already know the places you want to see. For many of our guests, that continuity is what makes the trip feel effortless.

It is a service for people who value their time and comfort: couples on honeymoon, families with children or older relatives, business travellers with packed schedules and visitors who would rather not depend on apps in a border city.

How it differs from taxis and ride-hailing apps

Taxis and apps solve one ride. A private driver solves the whole trip. In practice, the biggest differences are:

  • Scheduling: the car is at the door at the agreed time, with no need to call and hope a driver is available.
  • Fixed prices: you know the cost of each trip, or of the whole package, in advance.
  • Waiting included: on excursions, the driver waits while you visit, with no meter running.
  • Borders: our cars are ready to drive into Argentina and Paraguay, which not every app car is.
  • Luggage and shopping: bags stay safely in the car while you explore.

When a private driver makes the difference

Honeymoons and couples: enjoy the sights without maps, parking or worrying about getting back. Have wine with dinner without having to drive afterwards.

Families with older relatives or small children: the driver drops off and picks up at the door of each attraction, the child seat is already fitted and the car becomes your base for the day.

Business travel: meetings in Foz, Ciudad del Este and Puerto Iguazú on the same day, without wasting time finding transport in between.

Groups of friends: everyone in one 7-seat vehicle, with the route agreed in advance and the cost shared.

How we plan your schedule

It all starts with a conversation on WhatsApp. Tell us your dates, your hotel, how many people are travelling and what you would like to do. If you do not have a plan yet, we suggest one based on what works best in Foz: the falls early in the morning, shopping in Paraguay from Monday to Saturday and the Triple Frontier Landmark at sunset.

Once the schedule is set, we send you a fixed price. During your stay, changes are handled over the same WhatsApp chat. If it rains and you want to move the falls to another day, we rearrange everything.

Depending on your plans, the service can combine one-way transfers, full or half days and hourly bookings, always looking for the combination that works out best for you.

Vehicles

We work with executive cars with air conditioning and space for luggage. The most common options are:

  • Executive sedan, such as the Corolla: ideal for couples and business travellers, up to four passengers.
  • SUV, such as the Haval or the SW4: more room inside and a bigger boot, great for families.
  • 7-seat vehicle, such as the Spin: for larger families and groups of friends.

When you book, we recommend the right vehicle for your group size and luggage. If you have specific needs, such as a folding wheelchair, a pushchair or camera equipment, just let us know.

Languages and service for international guests

Foz do Iguaçu welcomes visitors from all over the world, and many arrive without speaking Portuguese. We answer messages in English, Spanish and Portuguese, and can assign an English-speaking driver, subject to availability on your dates.

For international travellers, a private driver helps with more than transport: guidance at the border crossings, where to exchange money safely and how tickets for each attraction work.

If you are arranging travel for a client, an executive or an international group, we can deal directly with you and keep the passenger informed throughout the stay. Payment can be made in Brazilian reais or US dollars.
