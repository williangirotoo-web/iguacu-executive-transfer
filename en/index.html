How it works

Executive transfer with a set time and a fixed price

In Foz do Iguaçu, most travellers need a ride from the airport, to Iguazu Falls or across the bridge to Argentina or Paraguay. With us, the ride is booked in advance: you choose the destination and the vehicle, see the price, and the driver is there at the agreed time.

The price is per vehicle, not per person, and it does not change with traffic, border queues or the time of day. At the airport, the driver tracks your flight and waits at arrivals with a sign with your name.

We operate 24/7 with booking, including late-night flights and holidays. Online booking works at any time; on WhatsApp we reply from 6 am to 11 pm (Brasília time).

Questions

Questions about transfers and taxis in Foz do Iguaçu

How much is a taxi from Foz do Iguaçu Airport to my hotel?

From R$ 350 per vehicle to a hotel in Foz do Iguaçu, for the whole group, with 1 hour of free waiting after landing. The price is fixed when you book.

Do you operate 24 hours a day?

Yes, with booking. We run transfers 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays and late-night flights.

Is the price per person or per vehicle?

Per vehicle, for the whole group. An airport transfer for 4 people costs the same as for 1.

Can you take me to Argentina and Paraguay?

Yes. Your driver crosses the border with you to Puerto Iguazú, the Argentine side of the Falls and Ciudad del Este. Bring your passport and check whether your nationality needs a visa for Argentina or Paraguay.

Do your drivers speak English?

Tell us your language when you book and we confirm a driver who speaks English, subject to availability on your date. Our WhatsApp team also replies in English.

How do I book?

On the booking page you choose the service, date and vehicle and see the price instantly. We confirm availability on WhatsApp and send a payment link.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

After we confirm availability, we send a payment link on WhatsApp: Pix, card or PayPal. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before.
