Why book in advance

VIP taxi or a regular taxi?

A regular taxi charges by the meter and you do not choose the car. With a VIP transfer, the price is fixed before you travel, the vehicle category is confirmed at booking and the driver waits for you at the agreed place.

Tell us your language when you book and we confirm a driver who speaks English, subject to availability on your date.

Questions

Questions about VIP taxi in Foz do Iguaçu

How much is a VIP transfer from the airport?

From R$ 1.200 in an Executive SUV and R$ 5.000 in a Luxury SUV, per vehicle, from Foz do Iguaçu Airport to your hotel, with 1 hour of free waiting.

Which cars are in the Luxury category?

SW4, Trailblazer or Commander. The category you book is guaranteed; the exact model depends on the fleet on the day.

Do the cars have Wi-Fi?

Yes. Executive Sedan, Executive SUV, Luxury and Executive Van cars have Starlink Wi-Fi on board. The 7-Seater has 5G/LTE mobile internet.

Does the driver speak English?

Tell us your language when you book and we confirm a driver who speaks English, subject to availability on your date.

Can I book a VIP car for a whole day?

Yes. A full day with driver, 10 hours and 200 km, costs from R$ 1.900 in an Executive SUV and R$ 9.200 in a Luxury SUV.

Do you issue invoices for companies?

Yes. Give us the company name and tax ID when you book.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

After we confirm availability, we send a payment link on WhatsApp: Pix, card or PayPal. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before.
