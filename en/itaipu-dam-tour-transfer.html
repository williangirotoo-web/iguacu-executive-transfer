Why Itaipu is worth a visit

Itaipu Binacional is a hydroelectric dam built jointly by Brazil and Paraguay on the Paraná River. It is one of the largest power plants in the world by energy production, and the scale is hard to grasp until you stand next to it: a wall of concrete and steel stretching for kilometres, with a reservoir behind it that looks more like a sea than a lake.

Even travellers with no interest in engineering usually leave impressed. The visit tells the story of how two countries built the project together, and the views over the dam and the reservoir are spectacular, especially on a clear day.

It is also an easy half-day trip. The visitor centre is a short drive north of downtown Foz do Iguaçu, so it fits well on the same day as the Triple Frontier Landmark or a city tour.

Which tour to choose

Itaipu offers several visit options. The two most popular are:

  • Panoramic Tour: a bus tour along the dam with stops at viewpoints. It is the most accessible option and works well for families and anyone with limited time.
  • Special Circuit: a more detailed technical visit that takes you inside the structure. It has a smaller group size and stricter rules, so book it in advance.

There are also other experiences, such as the evening illumination of the dam on selected nights and visits to the ecomuseum and the wildlife refuge. Schedules and prices change throughout the year, so check the official Itaipu Turismo website before your visit. We can help you fit the tour time into your day.

How the transfer works

You tell us your hotel and the time of your tour, and we plan the pick-up so you arrive with time to spare. The driver takes you to the visitor centre, waits while you do the tour and drives you back to your hotel, or on to your next stop.

The price is agreed before the trip and includes the waiting time. There is no meter running while you are inside the dam, and you do not have to look for a taxi when the tour finishes.

Bags and shopping can stay in the car during the visit. If you are coming straight from the airport or heading there afterwards, we can combine the trips.

Combine Itaipu with other sights

Itaipu sits on the same side of the city as several other attractions, which makes it easy to build a full day:

  • Itaipu in the afternoon and the Triple Frontier Landmark at sunset: our most requested combination.
  • Iguazu Falls in the morning and Itaipu after lunch: a long but very rewarding day if you are short on time.
  • Buddhist Temple and Itaipu: a calm, scenic half day that suits older travellers.

If you want all of this in one go, our private city tour puts the stops in the right order for you.

Practical tips for your visit

  • Bring your passport. Itaipu asks visitors for identification, especially on the more technical tours.
  • Book tickets online in high season, mainly July, December and January.
  • Wear comfortable, closed shoes if you are doing the Special Circuit. Some tours have dress rules.
  • Bring sunscreen and water in summer. Temperatures in Foz often go above 30 °C.
  • Allow about two to three hours in total, including the drive and the tour.
