How it works

A shopping day without carrying your bags

Ciudad del Este, in Paraguay, is just across the Friendship Bridge from Foz do Iguaçu. The traffic on the bridge can be slow, so we suggest leaving early in the morning. The driver drops you at the shopping area, waits up to 5 hours and keeps your purchases in the car while you continue.

Shops in Ciudad del Este open Monday to Saturday and most close in the late afternoon; on Sundays and public holidays almost everything is closed. Bring your passport. Citizens of Mercosur countries can use their national ID card. Check whether your nationality needs a visa for Paraguay or Argentina.

Customs

What to know before you go

Travellers entering Brazil have a duty-free allowance for goods bought in Paraguay by land, per person and per month. Check the current limit and rules with the Brazilian Federal Revenue (Receita Federal) before shopping, and keep your receipts.

Questions

Questions about the Ciudad del Este shopping trip

How much is a shopping trip to Ciudad del Este?

From R$ 1.200 in a Standard car, per vehicle and not per person, with the round trip and up to 5 hours of waiting. Extra hours cost R$ 100 per hour in a Standard car and R$ 125 in an Executive Sedan.

What documents do I need?

Bring your passport. Citizens of Mercosur countries can use their national ID card. Check whether your nationality needs a visa for Paraguay or Argentina.

When are the shops open?

Monday to Saturday, from the morning to the late afternoon. On Sundays and public holidays most shops are closed.

Can you pick me up at Foz do Iguaçu Airport and go straight to Ciudad del Este?

Yes. The airport transfer to Ciudad del Este costs from R$ 500 per vehicle.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

After we confirm availability, we send a payment link on WhatsApp: Pix, card or PayPal. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before.
