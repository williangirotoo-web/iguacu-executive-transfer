Itinerary

How the day usually goes

  • Morning: Friendship Bridge to Ciudad del Este, Paraguay, for shopping.
  • Afternoon: Tancredo Neves Bridge to Puerto Iguazú, Argentina: the Three Borders viewpoint, the craft market and Argentine food.
  • End of day: back in Brazil for the sunset at the Triple Frontier Landmark.

Leave early: the bridge to Paraguay is busiest in the middle of the day. Shops in Ciudad del Este are closed on Sundays and public holidays. Bring your passport. Citizens of Mercosur countries can use their national ID card. Check whether your nationality needs a visa for Paraguay or Argentina.

Questions

Questions about the three countries tour

How much is the three countries in one day tour?

From R$ 2.200 in a Standard car, per vehicle and not per person, with up to 8 hours of driver and both border crossings. Extra hours cost R$ 100 per hour in a Standard car and R$ 125 in an Executive Sedan.

What documents do I need?

Bring your passport. Citizens of Mercosur countries can use their national ID card. Check whether your nationality needs a visa for Paraguay or Argentina.

Can I do it on a Sunday?

You can visit Argentina and the Landmark, but most shops in Ciudad del Este are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

After we confirm availability, we send a payment link on WhatsApp: Pix, card or PayPal. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before.
