The day

How the day usually goes

  • Early morning: pick-up at your hotel and border crossing to Argentina.
  • Morning to early afternoon: 4 to 5 hours at Iguazú National Park: the circuits and the Devil's Throat walkway.
  • Afternoon: back to Brazil and 2 to 3 hours on the Brazilian side, with the panoramic view of the falls.

Why start in Argentina? It is the side that takes longer and involves more walking. Starting early means shorter queues, leaving the shorter Brazilian side for the afternoon.

Good to know

One day or two?

It is a full day. If you prefer to take it slowly, book each side on a different day. Bring your passport. Citizens of Mercosur countries can use their national ID card. Check whether your nationality needs a visa for Argentina or Brazil.

Questions

Questions about the both-sides tour

How much is the tour of both sides of the Falls?

From R$ 1.300 in a Standard car, per vehicle and not per person, with up to 8 hours of driver and the border crossings. Park tickets are paid separately.

Is there enough time to see both sides in one day?

Yes, if you leave early: 4 to 5 hours on the Argentine side and 2 to 3 hours on the Brazilian side, plus the border.

What if we go over 8 hours?

Extra hours cost R$ 100 per hour in a Standard car and R$ 125 in an Executive Sedan, and can be added when booking or arranged with the driver on the day.

Can you pick us up in Puerto Iguazú?

Yes. Add your hotel address when booking; for departures from Argentina we confirm the price on WhatsApp.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

After we confirm availability, we send a payment link on WhatsApp: Pix, card or PayPal. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before.
