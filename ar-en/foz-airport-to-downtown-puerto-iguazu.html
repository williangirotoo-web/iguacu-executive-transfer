How it works

From arrivals in Foz to downtown Puerto Iguazú

Foz do Iguaçu International Airport sits on the road to the Falls, close to the bridge to Argentina. Because it has more flights, many travelers staying in Puerto Iguazú fly into Foz. Your driver waits at the arrivals exit with a name sign and helps with the luggage.

From the airport we take the Tancredo Neves Bridge, go through Brazilian and Argentine immigration and drive straight to downtown Puerto Iguazú, home to the parrilla restaurants, shops, the Feirinha market and many hotels and Airbnbs. At quiet times the whole trip takes 40 to 60 minutes; on holidays and in high season the border can take longer.

Before you go

Flight delays, documents and address

Add your flight number when booking: the driver tracks your arrival time. The first hour after landing is included; after that, each extra hour is R$ 80.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much is a transfer from Foz Airport to downtown Puerto Iguazú?

From R$ 500 per car, not per person, with the border crossing and the first hour of waiting after landing included. The price is fixed when you book.

How long does it take from the airport to downtown Puerto Iguazú?

40 to 60 minutes at quiet times, including immigration. It can take longer on holidays and in high season.

What if my flight is delayed?

Your driver tracks the flight using the number you add when booking. The first hour after landing is included; after that, each extra hour is R$ 80.

Can you take us to an Airbnb or apartment downtown?

Yes. Add the full address when booking or send your location on WhatsApp, and the driver drops you at the door.

Do I need a passport?

Most visitors do. Mercosur citizens can use their national ID card. A driver's license is not accepted at the border.

Do you also go from downtown Puerto Iguazú to Foz Airport?

Yes, at the same price per car. Choose the direction when booking and add your flight time; we suggest leaving with extra time for the border.

I land late at night. Is there a transfer?

Yes. We operate 24 hours with advance booking.

How do I pay and can I cancel?

You pay when you book on the website, with an international card or PayPal (charged in Brazilian reais). Cancellation is free up to 48 hours before.
