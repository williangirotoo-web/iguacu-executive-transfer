How it works

From your door in Foz to your door in Argentina

Many travelers spend their first days in Foz do Iguaçu and then move to Puerto Iguazú to see the Argentine side of the Falls, the Feirinha market and the restaurants. With a transfer you skip street taxis with heavy bags and you never change cars at the border.

Your driver picks you up at your hotel, Airbnb or any address in Foz, takes the Tancredo Neves Bridge, goes through Brazilian and Argentine immigration with you and drops you at the door. At quiet times the whole trip takes 30 to 50 minutes; on holidays and in high season the border can take longer.

Before you go

Documents and address

Most visitors need a passport to enter Argentina. Mercosur citizens can use their national ID card. Minors need their own documents.

If you are staying at an Airbnb, add the full address when booking or send the location on WhatsApp, so the driver goes straight to your door.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a transfer from Foz do Iguaçu to Puerto Iguazú?

From R$ 500 per car, not per person, border crossing included. The price is fixed when you book.

Can you pick us up at an Airbnb or private house?

Yes. Add the full address when booking or send your location on WhatsApp.

How long does it take from Foz to Puerto Iguazú?

30 to 50 minutes at quiet times, including immigration. It can take longer on holidays and in high season.

Do I need a passport?

Most visitors do. Mercosur citizens can use their national ID card. A driver's license is not accepted at the border.

Can you also take us back from Puerto Iguazú to Foz?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with the date and address, or book the transfer from your Puerto Iguazú hotel to Foz Airport.

How do I pay and can I cancel?

You pay when you book on the website, with an international card or PayPal (charged in Brazilian reais). Cancellation is free up to 48 hours before.
