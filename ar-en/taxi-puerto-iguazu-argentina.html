How it works

Booked rides, not street taxis

We work with booked rides: you choose the route, the date and the vehicle, see the price and we confirm on WhatsApp. At the airport, the driver waits at arrivals with a sign with your name.

Crossing between Puerto Iguazú and Foz do Iguaçu takes the Tancredo Neves Bridge, usually 30 to 60 minutes depending on the border queue. Bring your passport. Citizens of Mercosur countries can use their national ID card. Check whether your nationality needs a visa for Argentina or Brazil.
