Questions about VIP transfers in Puerto Iguazú

How much is a VIP transfer from Foz Airport to Puerto Iguazú?

From R$ 1.300 in an Executive SUV and R$ 5.000 in a Luxury SUV, per vehicle, with the border crossing and 1 hour of free waiting.

Which cars are in the Luxury category?

SW4, Trailblazer or Commander. The category is guaranteed; the model depends on the fleet on the day.

Do the cars have Wi-Fi?

Yes. Executive Sedan, Executive SUV, Luxury and Executive Van cars have Starlink Wi-Fi on board. The 7-Seater has 5G/LTE mobile internet.

Does the driver speak English?

Tell us your language when you book and we confirm a driver who speaks English, subject to availability on your date.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

After we confirm availability, we send a payment link on WhatsApp: Pix, card or PayPal. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before.
