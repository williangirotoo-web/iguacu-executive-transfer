How it works

From arrivals in Brazil to your hotel in Argentina

Add your flight number when you book. The driver follows your flight and waits at the arrivals exit with a sign with your name. The Tancredo Neves Bridge is a few minutes from the airport; the driver stops at Brazilian and Argentine immigration with you and continues to your hotel in Puerto Iguazú.

The time at the border varies with the day and the hour. Bring your passport. Citizens of Mercosur countries can use their national ID card. Check whether your nationality needs a visa for Argentina or Brazil.

Which airport?

Foz do Iguaçu (IGU) or Puerto Iguazú (IGR)?

Both airports serve the Falls. Foz do Iguaçu (IGU), in Brazil, usually has more flights; Puerto Iguazú (IGR), in Argentina, is the choice for domestic flights from Buenos Aires. We pick you up at either one.

Questions

Questions about the Foz Airport to Puerto Iguazú transfer

How much is a transfer from Foz do Iguaçu Airport to Puerto Iguazú?

From R$ 500 per vehicle, for the whole group, with the border crossing and 1 hour of free waiting after landing. The same price applies from Puerto Iguazú to the airport.

How long does it take?

Usually 30 to 60 minutes, depending on the queue at the border.

What documents do I need?

Bring your passport. Citizens of Mercosur countries can use their national ID card. Check whether your nationality needs a visa for Argentina or Brazil.

Do you also pick up at Puerto Iguazú Airport (IGR)?

Yes. IGR to a hotel in Puerto Iguazú costs from R$ 500, and IGR to a hotel in Foz do Iguaçu from R$ 350, per vehicle.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

After we confirm availability, we send a payment link on WhatsApp: Pix, card or PayPal. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before.
