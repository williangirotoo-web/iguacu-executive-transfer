The Argentine side

What to expect at Iguazú National Park

The Argentine side is where you get closest to the water: jungle trails, the Upper and Lower Circuits and the walkway over the Devil's Throat, reached by the park's ecological train. Most visitors need 4 to 5 hours, which is why the round trip includes up to 5 hours of waiting.

Leave early to beat the queues at the border and at the park entrance. Bring your passport (Mercosur citizens can use their national ID card) and check whether your nationality needs a visa for Argentina.

Brazilian or Argentine side?

Which side of the Falls should you visit?

The Brazilian side gives you the panoramic view of the falls in 2 to 3 hours. The Argentine side is longer and more immersive. If you have the time, see both: on different days or on the same day with our both-sides tour, starting with Argentina in the morning.

Questions

Questions about the Iguazu Falls Argentina transfer

How much is a transfer from Foz do Iguaçu to the Argentine Falls?

A round trip with up to 5 hours of waiting costs from R$ 1.300, and one way from R$ 425, per vehicle and not per person, from your hotel in Foz do Iguaçu.

What documents do I need to cross into Argentina?

A valid passport. Citizens of Mercosur countries can use their national ID card. Check whether your nationality needs a visa for Argentina before you travel.

How long does the visit to the Argentine side take?

Most visitors spend 4 to 5 hours, including the ecological train, the circuits and the Devil's Throat walkway.

What if I stay longer than 5 hours?

Extra hours can be added when you book or arranged with the driver on the day.

Are park tickets included?

No. The transfer includes the car, the driver, fuel, the border crossing and the waiting time. Park tickets are paid separately.

Can you pick me up at a hotel in Puerto Iguazú?

Yes. Add your hotel address when you book; for departures from Argentina we confirm the price on WhatsApp.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

After we confirm availability, we send a payment link on WhatsApp: Pix, card or PayPal. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before.
